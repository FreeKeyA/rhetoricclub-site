RHETORIC Club

Homeschool Speech and Debate

RHETORIC Club is a homeschool speech and debate club.

RHETORIC Club is a program of RHETORIC LTD, a registered nonprofit organization. The domain rhetoricclub.org is owned and operated by RHETORIC LTD for club email and communications.

Our Mission

RHETORIC equips Christian home educating families through speech and debate, building on the work of the local Church to prepare students to defend the Faith and fulfill their callings, understanding that Godly rhetoric is not only about skill, but also rooted in the mind and heart. RHETORIC is an Indiana nonprofit affiliated with Christian Communicators of America (CCA).
